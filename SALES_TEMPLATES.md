"This is the live pricing dashboard. It shows current market rates for 8 metal types—copper, aluminum, steel, brass, and others. These prices update daily from verified sources. You can see exactly what the market is paying right now."

*Point out copper at $3.45/lb, aluminum at $0.85/lb, etc.*

*Show the comparison table. Highlight metals where they're below market.*

## Objection Handling

### "We already know our market prices."
**Response**: "That's great. But market prices change daily. Ferrum AI updates automatically, so you don't have to. Plus, it calculates your exact revenue leakage—most yards are surprised by the number."

### "How accurate are your prices?"
**Response**: "We pull from verified sources like Rockaway Recycling, iScrap App, and ISRI. We cross-reference multiple sources to ensure accuracy. You can see the sources in the dashboard."

### "What if we don't like it?"
**Response**: "No problem. You can cancel anytime. But I'm confident that once you see your real revenue leakage, you'll want to stay. Most yards recover their subscription cost in the first month."

### "How much does it cost?"
**Response**: "Our standard pricing is $199-$1,499/month depending on the tier. But as a Founding Partner, you get 50% off for 12 months, plus your price is locked for life. So you'd be at [50% of tier price] for the first year, then that rate never increases."

**Response**: "Absolutely. But here's the thing—every day you're not using Ferrum AI, you're leaving money on the table. How about I send you a link to create an account? You can see your real leakage number, and then we can talk next steps. No pressure."
